Overview

This comprehensive professional learning initiative translates abstract educational theory into practical classroom habits. Stepping in to rescue a stalled project, I transformed fragmented resources into an engaging, high-completion Canvas LMS module. The experience features interactive H5P branching scenarios that place educators in the driver's seat of simulated classroom dialogues, alongside polished, highly scaffolded instructional videos filmed live in a primary school setting.

Part 1: Instructional Design & Project Rescue
1. Strategic Alignment & Course Recovery
  • Project Rescue: Audited abandoned course drafts, restructured learning pathways, and established clear production milestones to bring the stalled initiative to completion.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Collaborated with school leaders and teaching staff through influence and clear communication, scouting core content while keeping teams aligned without direct authority.
  • LMS Architecture: Rebuilt the Canvas module structure to ensure intuitive navigation, clean typography, and seamless mobile accessibility under strict institutional guidelines.
Design by Kylie Baxter
2. Pedagogical Design & Interactive Branching (H5P)
  • WalkThrus Framework Integration: Operationalised Tom Sherrington’s WalkThrus model, breaking down complex questioning techniques into step-by-step visual guides for teachers.
  • Simulated Classroom Dialogues: Engineered a "Choose Your Own Adventure" branching scenario in H5P. Educators use targeted probing questions to navigate realistic student personas (e.g., "The Developing Learner"), testing different pedagogical approaches (definition vs. function).
  • Strategic Reinforcement: Incorporated "inner monologue" screens that reveal the rationale behind each instructional choice, paired with active recall checks embedded throughout the course.
Part 2: Featured Deliverable – "Think, Pair, Share" Video Production

The Objective: To produce a clear, engaging instructional video demonstrating Tom Sherrington’s "Think, Pair, Share" WalkThru as an explicit, applicable model for educators. This required embedding seamlessly into the broader Canvas professional learning module.

The Challenge: Live-Environment Logistics & Pedagogical Direction. Filming in a live educational environment requires a delicate balance of capturing authentic moments while actively translating educational theory into visual narrative:

  • Live Pedagogical Observation: Observed and filmed the live Year 1 classroom simultaneously, independently identifying and mapping the micro-features of Sherrington’s WalkThru in action to ensure the captured footage aligned precisely with the course's theoretical framework.
  • Unobtrusive Classroom Capture: Maintained a strict "fly-on-the-wall" approach to minimise classroom disruptions, while gracefully managing curious young students to preserve a professional lesson flow.
  • Media Compliance & Scheduling: Navigated complex logistics, including scheduling around principal directives (e.g., avoiding sports uniform days) and managing media releases. Students without filming permissions were seamlessly relocated prior to the shoot.
  • Interview Prompting & Subject Coaching: During the sit-down interview, I drew directly upon my real-time classroom observations to prompt the teacher. By anchoring the discussion in specific moments captured on camera, I helped him break past initial nerves and impostor syndrome. This active direction shaped an authentic, organic performance that positioned him as a confident, highly developed expert on screen.
The Process & Technical Execution
Design by Kylie Baxter

(Think, Pair, Share: Illustration of Practice - Premiere Pro Timeline)

The multi-track Premiere Pro timeline illustrates the complexity and organisation of the post-production workflow:

  • Complex Multi-Track Management: Utilised multiple video (V1–V3) and audio (A1–A3) tracks to layer the instructor's presentation, b-roll of the classroom environment, and visual aids.
  • Meticulous Organisation: Applied a strict colour-coding system to timeline clips to categorise footage (e.g., A-roll, student interaction, graphics), ensuring an efficient and error-free editing workflow.
  • Pedagogical Pacing & Student Integration: Carefully manipulated audio tracks and utilised J/L cuts to ensure smooth transitions during student interaction segments. Preserving and highlighting genuine student responses was critical, providing teachers with a true-to-life, exemplary model of classroom questioning in action.
  • Rapid Asset Pipeline: Leveraged ElevenLabs AI audio synthesis for fast, high-fidelity professional voiceovers, and dynamic Premiere Pro transcript generation for highly accurate accessibility captioning.
The Final Result
Design by Kylie Baxter

("Illustrations of Practice Interactive" UI screenshot)

The finalised video assets were seamlessly integrated into an H5P interactive layout within the "Think, Pair, Share" Canvas course page. The user-friendly interface featured intuitive dual-button navigation, allowing educators to access both the primary instructional video and a secondary resource video, supported by fully accessible transcripts and subtitles. The clean, professional edit ensured that the core message—demonstrating practical classroom implementation to help Sydney Catholic schools build student confidence and foster a 100% participation culture—remained the focal point of the learning experience.
