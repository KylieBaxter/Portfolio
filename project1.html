Overview

Delivering an accredited professional learning sequence co-developed by Sydney Catholic Schools and ACU’s Australian Centre for the Advancement of Literacy required a refined, highly reliable production pipeline built on close collaboration between our production team and their academic stakeholders.

1. High-Stakes Partnership, Cross-Team Collaboration & Asset Management

Collaborative production pipeline and asset management strategies implemented for the course:

  • Collaborative Production Pipeline: Co-ordinated seamlessly between our creative team and ACU/SCS subject matter experts, shifting production from SME self-editing to a structured raw-file intake workflow to establish complete control over pacing, narrative flow, and broadcast-quality output.
  • Specialised Audio Restoration: Executed multi-track restoration and advanced noise reduction in Adobe Audition to clean up varied room acoustics and complex speaker profiles where automated AI tools fell short.
  • Brand & Accreditation Standards: Designed custom slide templates and visual iconography that strictly adhered to both ACU academic guidelines and Sydney Catholic Schools identity standards.
Before by Kylie Baxter
2. Micro-Learning Architecture & Pedagogical Engagement for Digital Cohorts

Designed for NSW educators completing mandatory maintenance for Proficient Teacher Accreditation, transforming dense academic lectures into high-retention digital learning modules:

  • Optimised Runtime Scaling & Cognitive Load Theory (45–60 Mins → 15–30 Mins): Applied cognitive load theory and micro-learning pacing principles to condense verbose lecture streams into tight, high-impact learning bites optimised for digital cohorts, supporting flexible professional learning and exceptional student experience goals.
  • Visual Synthesis & Conceptual Logic: Translated intricate linguistic and cognitive frameworks—such as the intersection of semantics, phonology, and orthography—into progressive visual layouts and step-by-step graphic structures.
  • Scannable Iconography: Designed recognisable visual icons for abstract phonics and literacy concepts, making key takeaways instantly digestible for busy educators.
Design by Kylie Baxter
3. Authentic On-Location Field Production, Editorial Scaffolding & Quality Control

Bridging high-level university literacy research with practical classroom execution across New South Wales through sophisticated post-production and quality control:

  • Classroom Demonstration Filming: Conducted on-location video production in active K–2 classrooms within Sydney Catholic Schools, capturing literacy academics demonstrating explicit phonics strategies live with students.
  • Dynamic Contextual Pop-Ups & Multi-Speaker Scaffolding: Engineered synchronised on-screen graphic callouts and picture-in-picture (PiP) face pop-ups for secondary experts, enabling seamless real-time interjections, nuance corrections, and pedagogical meta-analysis without disrupting instructional momentum.
  • Quality Control, Accessibility & Transcript Management: Managed comprehensive post-production quality control standards, overseeing platform-specific delivery, closed captions, and complete transcript management to ensure full accessibility compliance.
*Note: Classroom demonstration footage is featured in brief, excerpted clips with student faces privacy-masked in strict compliance with Sydney Catholic Schools and ACU media governance and staff privacy policies. Proprietary course assets remain restricted; timeline layouts, visual design specs, and audio restoration workflows represent the production methodology.
